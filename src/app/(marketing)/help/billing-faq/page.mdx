# Billing and plans FAQ

## Plan ladder

ProcuLink offers five plans sized to your supplier flow volume:

- `Pilot` — free for 14 days; 20 orders total during trial; 1 supplier.
- `Growth` — €149/month; 150 orders/month; 5 suppliers.
- `Operations` — €399/month; 500 orders/month; 10 suppliers.
- `Integration` — €999/month; 1,000 orders/month; 20 suppliers.
- `Enterprise` — custom pricing from €2,500/month; contact sales for custom volume, ERP connectors, and SLA.

## What happens at quota

When you reach the monthly order limit on a paid plan, new uploads return a `429` response and the dashboard shows an upgrade banner. Orders that were already uploaded continue to parse, validate, transform, and deliver normally — only new uploads are paused. Your quota resets automatically on your billing anniversary.

## When Pilot ends

After 14 days or 20 orders (whichever comes first), the `Pilot` workspace becomes read-only. You can still view previous orders, mappings, outputs, and billing history, but you cannot upload new files, transform documents, deliver to suppliers, or add suppliers until you upgrade to `Growth` or higher. Upgrading restores full access immediately — no data is lost.

## How to cancel

Open `Settings → Billing → Manage subscription` to launch the Stripe Customer Portal. From there you can cancel your subscription, switch between `Growth`, `Operations`, and `Integration`, update your card details, or download past invoices. `Enterprise` changes are handled directly through your account manager.
