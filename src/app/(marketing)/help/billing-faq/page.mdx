# Plans and billing

What each plan includes, what counts as a document, and what happens at the limit.

## What counts as a document

A document is one file of up to 10 pages. A longer file counts once for every 10 pages. Only pages we read count, and we read up to 100 pages of a file.

## The plans

<PlanTable />

The {PLAN_BY_ID.enterprise.name} plan has a volume and a price agreed with you. [Talk to us](/book-demo).

Every paid plan has every way to send documents in, every file type and every check, and any number of users. Plans differ only by how many documents you process and how many flows you set up.

## What happens at the limit

On a paid plan you are never blocked for volume. Extra documents are still read and sent as normal. They are billed at your plan's price for an extra document, on your next invoice. Up to the volume of the largest plan, you are never charged more than the cheapest plan that would cover that month. **Settings** warns you as you near the limit and when you pass it. Your allowance starts again with each billing month.

Flows are different. When you reach your plan's flow limit, you cannot add another flow until you change plan.

## When the trial ends

The trial lasts {trial.priceCadence} and includes {trial.orderLimit} documents. Once you have used them, new documents aren't accepted, but you can still send the ones you have until the trial's last day. When the trial ends, your workspace becomes read-only. You can still open and export everything, but new documents aren't accepted until you choose a plan.

To continue, open **Settings**, go to **Plan & billing** and choose a plan. Nothing you set up is lost.

## Changing plan

Only a workspace admin can change plan. In **Plan & billing**, choose **Monthly** or **Annual** under **Billing period**, then a plan under **Change plan**. On a paid plan, an upgrade asks you to confirm, charges the card on file today, and credits the unused part of your current plan. **Manage in Stripe** opens your card, billing email and invoices.

If you are on {LEGACY_PAID_PLANS.map((p) => p.name).join(", ")}, nothing changes until you change plan. You keep your current price and limits. When you change plan, you choose from the plans above.

## If a payment fails

If your card is declined, **Plan & billing** shows "Your last payment didn't go through." New documents are not accepted until the payment clears. Select **Manage in Stripe** to update your card.

## How to cancel

Open **Settings**, go to **Plan & billing**, and select **Manage in Stripe**. In Stripe you can cancel, update your card or download invoices.

**What cancelling does.** When the subscription ends, ProcuLink stops accepting new documents on every route: uploads, emailed documents, the file API and AI assistants stop, and mailbox, SFTP, FTP, FTPS and S3 pickups pause and leave your files where they are. Uploads, emails and API calls sent after that are not held for later, so tell your senders before you cancel. Everything already processed remains readable and exportable. Activity records such as delivery attempts are removed after 180 days ([Privacy Policy](/privacy)). Subscribing again turns processing back on at once, with your setup untouched.
