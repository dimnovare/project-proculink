<Prereqs>
- A flow whose documents are invoices or credit notes. See [Set up your first flow](/help/guides/set-up-your-first-flow).
- A workspace admin account. Only admins add and change files.
- For steps 2 to 6, early access switched on for your workspace. Until then, those formats and destinations are not offered.
</Prereqs>

<Step n={1} title="Make a ZUGFeRD or Factur-X invoice">

Open the flow, go to the **We send** step, make a file and choose **A standard format**. Pick ZUGFeRD / Factur-X. It is on for every workspace.

It is a PDF invoice people can read, with the invoice data inside as XML. The readable page is in the invoice's language: German, French, Polish or Estonian from the seller's country, otherwise English. The data inside it is the same in every language.

Send it like any other file: by email, to a server or a folder, or as a download. See [Send files to a destination](/help/guides/send-to-a-destination).

<Outcome>
Each invoice in the flow gives a ZUGFeRD / Factur-X PDF.
</Outcome>

</Step>
