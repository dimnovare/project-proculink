<Prereqs>
- {requiresPlan("emailIngestion")}. The form saves on Pilot, but the polling toggle is refused below that plan — and so is the hosted inbound address this guide offers as the shorter path, so neither email route is a way around it.
- A mailbox **you already run** that receives purchase orders as attachments, and permission to create credentials on it.
- At least one supplier added — imported messages have to be attributed to someone.
- Twenty minutes, mostly spent in your mail provider's security settings rather than in ProcuLink.
</Prereqs>

<Step n={1} title="Check you actually need IMAP">

Every workspace already has an address of its own — `your-workspace@orders.proculink.eu` — that needs no
mailbox, no password, and no configuration. If you can ask senders to use it, or forward to it, do that
instead: [receive orders by email](/help/guides/receive-orders-by-email) is the shorter path and there is
no credential to store or rotate.

IMAP polling is for the case where neither is acceptable: the orders must stay in a mailbox you control,
and you would rather not change how senders address their mail.

<Outcome>
You are here on purpose, having ruled out the option that needs no credentials.
</Outcome>

</Step>

<Step n={2} title="Generate an app password, not your login password">

Do this before you open ProcuLink, because it is the step that actually takes time.

Gmail and Microsoft 365 refuse plain IMAP sign-in with your normal password when two-factor or MFA is
switched on — which it usually is. You need a dedicated **app password**: a separate credential, scoped to
mail access, that can be revoked on its own without touching the account password.

- **Google:** account security settings → app passwords. Requires 2-Step Verification to be on first.
- **Microsoft 365 / Outlook:** account security settings → app passwords. Your tenant administrator may
  have disabled them, in which case they have to enable them for the account, or you use a different
  mailbox.

Generate it against the mailbox you are going to poll, and paste it straight into ProcuLink in step 4 —
these are usually shown once.

<IfThisFails>
If your organisation forbids app passwords outright, IMAP polling is not available to you on that
mailbox. Use the hosted `your-workspace@orders.proculink.eu` address with a forwarding rule instead — no
credential leaves your side at all.
</IfThisFails>

</Step>

<Step n={3} title="Find your provider's host and port">

| Provider | IMAP host | Port |
|---|---|---|
| Gmail / Google Workspace | `imap.gmail.com` | `993` |
| Microsoft 365 (business) | `outlook.office365.com` | `993` |
| Outlook.com (personal) | `imap-mail.outlook.com` | `993` |
| Anything else | Ask your provider or IT team | `993` |

Use `993` with **SSL** left on. That is IMAP over TLS and is correct for every provider above. Do not use
the older plaintext port `143`.

<IfThisFails>
**Proton** is only reachable over IMAP through the local Proton Bridge app,
which listens on `127.0.0.1:1143` on the machine running it. ProcuLink's poller runs on our servers, so it
cannot reach an instance of that app on your laptop. Proton works here only where Proton Bridge runs
on a host we can reach — otherwise use the hosted address.
</IfThisFails>

</Step>

<Step n={4} title="Fill in the IMAP mailbox form">

Open **Settings → Email intake**. Below the hosted address you will find **IMAP mailbox — unseen messages
with CSV, XLSX, or PDF attachments are imported.**

| Field | What to enter |
|---|---|
| IMAP host | From the table in step 3. Required. |
| Port | `993`, with **SSL** ticked. |
| Username | Usually the full email address, for example `orders@company.com`. Required. |
| Password | The app password from step 2 — not your login password. |
| Folder | `INBOX`, or another folder name to poll that one instead. |
| Default supplier | The supplier imported messages are attributed to when the document does not identify one. Required. |

<GuideShot
  name="imap-mailbox-form"
  alt="The IMAP mailbox form in Settings with host, port, SSL, username, password, folder, and default supplier fields, and a Save email button."
  caption="Settings → Email intake. Passwords are stored encrypted and the field never shows the stored value back."

Press **Save email**. The footer confirms what happens to the credential: *"Passwords are stored
encrypted."*

<IfThisFails>
If the **Default supplier** list reads *"No suppliers yet — add one first →"*, stop here and [add a
supplier](/help/guides/add-a-supplier). The form will not enable polling without one, because there would
be nothing to check imported orders against.
</IfThisFails>

</Step>

<Step n={5} title="Turn on Poll inbox for orders">

Switch on **Poll inbox for orders**. The line beside it changes from **Disabled** to **Checking every five
minutes**, which is the whole schedule — there is no faster or slower option.

What the poller does, precisely:

- Reads **unseen** messages in the chosen folder.
- Imports **CSV, XLSX, and PDF attachments**. Each attachment becomes its own order.
- Marks a message it has taken as `Seen`, so it is not imported twice.
- Never deletes or moves your mail.

<Outcome>
The toggle stays on after saving, and within five minutes any unread order mail already sitting in that
folder appears in your Inbox.
</Outcome>

<IfThisFails>
A toggle that will not move is the plan gate, and it says so: *"Email ingestion is included on every paid
plan. You can set it up here, but turning on polling needs a paid plan — the Pilot plan doesn't include
it."* Everything you entered is saved; polling begins when the plan changes.
</IfThisFails>

</Step>

<Step n={6} title="Send one test message and watch it land">

Send a real purchase order to that mailbox yourself, as an attachment, and leave it unread. Within about
five minutes it should appear in your Inbox with the right purchase-order number and lines.

<IfThisFails>
**Login rejected** is almost always the login password used where an app password is required — regenerate
one and retry. **Connects but imports nothing** means one of three things: the orders are in a different
folder, the messages carry no supported attachment, or the messages had already been marked read before
polling started. Per-provider detail is in [polling an existing mailbox with
IMAP](/help/imap-provider-setup).
</IfThisFails>

</Step>

<Step n={7} title="Notice when it stops">

An IMAP credential is a thing that expires. App passwords get rotated, revoked, or invalidated when the
account's security settings change — and when that happens, mail keeps arriving in your mailbox and simply
stops arriving in ProcuLink.

The tell is on the same screen: **Last poll**, at the bottom of the Email intake section. A timestamp that
stops advancing means the poller is no longer getting in. Check it whenever the Inbox looks quieter than
the mailbox.

Keep the folder narrow, too. Polling `INBOX` on a busy shared mailbox means every unread message with a
spreadsheet attached becomes an order. A dedicated folder, fed by a rule in your mail provider, keeps
ProcuLink looking only at mail that really is an order.

<Outcome>
Orders arrive from a mailbox you still own and control, and you know the one number to look at when they
stop.
</Outcome>

</Step>
