<Prereqs>
- A ProcuLink workspace you can sign in to.
- One document like the ones you will receive. A real one is best, or a copy with the private details blanked out. It can be a PDF, a scan or photo, a CSV, an Excel file, a Word file, a text file, XML or JSON, up to 10 MB. See [Files we read](/help/what-we-read).
- Nothing is sent while you follow this guide until you choose to in step 3.
</Prereqs>

<Step n={1} title="Add a document">

Open ProcuLink. On an empty workspace the first screen asks for one document like the ones you get every week: drop it, choose it, or on a phone take a photo of a paper document. No document ready? Use our sample invoice. It is marked as a sample, never counts against your plan, and is never sent anywhere.

<GuideShot name="add-a-document" alt="The welcome screen with a box to drop a document, a Choose a file button and a link to use our sample invoice." />

<IfThisFails>
If we could not read the file, the screen says why. Press **Try again** when it is offered, or use another file. See [Troubleshooting](/help/troubleshooting).
</IfThisFails>

</Step>

<Step n={2} title="Check what we read">

The original stays beside the values we found. A value we are unsure of is marked in amber with the reason; click it to correct it. Untick anything you don't need. We name the flow from the document; change the name if you like. Then press **Looks right, next**.

<GuideShot name="check-what-we-read" alt="Step 2 of the setup: the sample invoice on the left, and on the right the flow name and the values we read, each with a tick box." />

</Step>

<Step n={3} title="Choose where it goes">

Pick **Download** (nothing to set up: each file waits in Documents), **Email**, **Google Drive** or **Google Sheets** (when your workspace offers Google), **OneDrive** (when your workspace offers Microsoft), a place you saved before, or a server, S3 bucket or web address under **A server or another system**. A new destination is tested first. Then choose which new documents wait for you: **Check only what we marked**, **Check every document** or **Check nothing**.

Press **Finish**. With **Download**, your first file is made and waits in Documents. With a destination, send your document now or later from Documents. See [Destinations](/help/destinations) and [Make a file](/help/guides/build-an-output).

<GuideShot name="choose-where-it-goes" alt="Step 3 of the setup: the read sample invoice at the top, then the choices Download, Email and A server or another system, and which documents wait for you." />

</Step>

## Then send in your first document

Forward a document to the flow's email address, or upload one on the flow's **Documents in this flow**. When it has been read, open it and check it. See [Check and send a document](/help/guides/check-and-send-a-document).
