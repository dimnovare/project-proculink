<Prereqs>
- A ProcuLink workspace you can sign in to.
- One document like the ones you will receive. A real one is best, or a copy with the private details blanked out. It can be a PDF, a scan or photo, a CSV, an Excel file, a Word file, a text file, XML or JSON, up to 10 MB. See [Files we read](/help/what-we-read).
- Nothing is sent while you follow this guide until you choose to in step 3.
</Prereqs>

<Step n={1} title="Add a document">

Open ProcuLink. On an empty workspace the first screen asks for one document like the ones you get every week: drop it, choose it, or on a phone take a photo of a paper document. No document ready? Use our sample invoice. It is marked as a sample, never counts against your plan, and is never sent anywhere.

<IfThisFails>
If we could not read the file, the screen says why. Press **Try again** when it is offered, or use another file. See [Troubleshooting](/help/troubleshooting).
</IfThisFails>

</Step>

<Step n={2} title="Check what we read">

The original stays beside the values we found. A value we are unsure of is marked in amber with the reason; click it to correct it. Untick anything you don't need. We name the flow from the document; change the name if you like. Then press **Looks right, next**.

</Step>

<Step n={3} title="Choose where it goes">

Pick **Download** (nothing to set up: each file waits in Documents, ready to download), **Email**, **Google Drive** or **Google Sheets** (when your workspace offers Google), or a place you saved before under **Already set up**. A server (SFTP or FTPS), an S3 bucket and a web address are one press away, under **A server or another system**. A new destination is tested first: most get a small test file, and Google Sheets is only checked, with no rows added. Choose whether new documents wait for you (**Review before delivery**) or are sent as soon as they are read (**Automatic delivery**). Then press **Finish**: with **Download**, your first file is made and waits in Documents. With a destination, send your document now, or finish without sending and send it later from Documents.

Destinations are explained in [Destinations](/help/destinations). To change the file we make, see [Make a file](/help/guides/build-an-output).

</Step>

## Then send in your first document

Forward a document to the flow's email address, or upload one on the flow's **Documents** page. When it has been read, open it and check it. See [Check and send a document](/help/guides/check-and-send-a-document).
