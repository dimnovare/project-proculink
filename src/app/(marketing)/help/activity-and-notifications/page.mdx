# History and notifications

Where to see what happened to each document, and how ProcuLink tells you by email or on your phone when something needs you.

## One document's history

Open a document and select **History** on its screen. It lists each change with its date and time, in a few plain words, such as Value edited or Value taught. It is open to everyone, on every plan.

## History, for the whole workspace

The **History** link at the bottom of **Documents** lists what happened to every document in your workspace, newest first, grouped by day. Each row is one event, such as a document that arrived, was read, had a value changed, or was sent.

Two tabs choose what you see:

- **All**: every event, the documents we sent and the ones we couldn't send included.
- **Changes**: edited values, versions put in use and settings changes.

**Documents** at the top of the page takes you back.

Search looks at file names and document numbers. **Newer** and **Older** page through the list.

Open a row to see more. For a send that failed, it shows where the send stopped, the destination, how many attempts were made and the server's reply. **Fix the destination** opens the flow's files, where its destination is set, and **Send again** tries once more. A new row shows the result.

**Export** downloads the tab you are on, with your search, as a CSV file. It holds up to the newest 2,000 events, and says so when there were more.

### Who can open it

History is open to everyone in your workspace, on every plan, the trial included. It stays readable after a trial or a subscription has ended.

## Emails when something needs you

ProcuLink can email you, but only if you switch it on. It is off for everyone until they do, and it is a setting for each person, so a colleague's choice does not change yours.

Open **Settings**, go to **Workspace**, and find **Tell me when**. Under **By email** there are two boxes:

- **When documents are waiting for review**
- **When a document can't be read or sent**

Emails go to the address you sign in with. You get at most one every 15 minutes. Each email is a short summary: for each flow, how many documents are waiting for review, and how many couldn't be read or sent, with a link to open ProcuLink. It does not include file names or values from your documents.

A new switch starts counting from the moment you turn it on, so your first email does not list older documents. If nothing needs you in a 15-minute period, no email is sent.

Not every document triggers an email. The review emails count documents that were read and are waiting for you. The failure emails count documents that couldn't be read, and deliveries that couldn't be made.

To stop the emails, switch both off.

## Phone alerts

ProcuLink can also alert you on your phone, but only if you turn it on. Open **Settings**, go to **Workspace**, and find **Use ProcuLink like an app**. Press **Turn on phone alerts**, then allow notifications when your phone asks. It is a setting for each person and each device. Press **Send a test alert** to check that an alert reaches the device.

You get an alert when a document is waiting for your check, when a document couldn't be sent, and when the first document from a new email address waits for you. A new alert of the same kind replaces the last one, and you get at most one of each kind every 5 minutes. Each day at 16:00 UTC you also get a summary, such as 12 documents today, 2 need you, on days when documents arrived. Where your phone supports it, the ProcuLink icon shows how many documents need you.

Alerts never include file names, amounts or anything else from your documents: only how many, and a link to open ProcuLink.

Android phones get alerts in the browser or the installed app. On an iPhone, add ProcuLink to your home screen first (iOS 16.4 or later), then turn alerts on from the app on your home screen. If you belong to more than one workspace, a device gets alerts from the one you last turned them on in.

Press **Turn off phone alerts** to stop them on that device. Signing out also stops them on that device. If alerts stop reaching a device, Settings says so: turn them on again there.
