# Activity and notifications

Where to see what happened to each document, and how ProcuLink tells you when something needs you.

## One document's history

Open a document and select **History** on its screen. It lists each change with its date and time, in a few plain words, such as **Value edited** or **Value taught**. It is open to everyone, on every plan.

## Activity, for the whole workspace

**Activity** in the menu lists what happened to every document in your workspace, newest first. Each row is one event:

- **Document received**
- **Document read**
- **Couldn't read**
- **Read again**
- **Value changed**
- **Value taught**
- **Sent**
- **Delivered**
- **Couldn't send**

You can filter by kind of event, search, and page back through older events. **Export log** downloads what is on screen as a CSV file.

### Who can open it

Activity is open on every paid plan from Starter up, for as long as the subscription is active. On a Trial, and after a subscription has ended, Activity shows a notice that this page is included from the Starter plan up, with a **See plans** link. Everything else keeps working. One document's **History** is always there.

## Emails when something needs you

ProcuLink can email you, but only if you switch it on. It is off for everyone until they do, and it is a setting for each person, so a colleague's choice does not change yours.

Open **Settings**, go to **Notifications**, and find **Email me**. There are two switches:

- **When documents are waiting for review**
- **When a document can't be read or sent**

Emails go to the address you sign in with. You get at most one every 15 minutes. Each email is a short summary: for each flow, how many documents are waiting for review, and how many couldn't be read or sent, with a link to open ProcuLink. It does not include file names or values from your documents.

A new switch starts counting from the moment you turn it on, so your first email does not list older documents. If nothing needs you in a 15-minute period, no email is sent.

Not every document triggers an email. The review emails count documents that were read and are waiting for you. The failure emails count documents that couldn't be read, and deliveries that couldn't be made.

To stop the emails, switch both off.
